Workspace What is Noema? FAQ

FAQ

Will it just tell me the answer?

No, and not as a matter of taste — it is enforced. The reference panel in every animation is masked: where the correct matrix, determinant, eigenvalue or solution would be printed you see [? ?]. The hint is checked by a linter that rejects digits, spelled-out numbers, fractions, percentages, and even the difference or ratio between your value and the right one. It names a place to look and a thing to watch, and that is all.

What maths does it cover?

Linear algebra: matrix products and the order they are applied in, inverses and determinants, eigenvectors, and projections. It does not do calculus — there is no derivative, integral or limit anywhere in it, so asking for one gets you an honest no rather than a confident wrong answer.

Do I have to photograph my work?

No. You can type it, one step per line, and the same checks run: 

A  = [0 -1; 1 0]
B  = [3 0; 0 1]
AB = [0 -3; 1 0]

Products written as AB or A*B, sums, det(A), inverses as A^-1 or inv(A), and dot products all parse. Entries can be integers, decimals or fractions.

Can I upload a PDF?

Not yet. The extractor opens every upload as an image, so a PDF comes back as an extraction error. Export the page as PNG or JPEG. The picker accepts PDFs and warns you rather than failing silently, but the warning is the honest state of it.

What if it misreads my handwriting?

A line it cannot read is not treated as a mistake. A step counts as wrong only if every plausible reading of it, against every plausible reading of the givens, is wrong. Work you crossed out yourself is never held against you, and writing 0.71 for 1/√2 is a rounding, not an error.

My method is different from the textbook's. Will it mark me wrong?

It should not. Each step is checked against the original given data, never against Noema's own solution path, precisely because your route through a problem is allowed to differ. Only the first failing step is blamed — later arithmetic that faithfully carries a bad number forward is not counted against you.

Is my work graded, or kept?

It is not graded. Nothing you upload is written to a database: analyses live in the server's memory and the oldest are dropped once there are more than a couple of hundred. Rendered videos are cached as files so a repeat is instant. If the server is running in live mode, your photograph is sent to a vision model to be transcribed — that is how the handwriting is read.

It showed me a problem I never uploaded. Why?

The server was in fixture mode, which needs no API key and replays bundled samples. In that mode your photo is not read at all and a sample is shown instead. It says so in red above the animation when it happens; if you see that, the work on screen is not yours.

The animation did not render.

Rendering needs Manim, plus ffmpeg and cairo, installed on the machine running the server. If those are missing every analysis still works — the step is still located and the hint still appears — but the video panel reports the failure instead of playing.
